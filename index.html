Lucas's AI trading bot · live trial

Virtual money only · last update 2026-11-08 19:15 (Madrid) · updates every hour

I designed and trained an AI trading agent, built with Claude, that manages a virtual eToro account on its own. It has one year to show it can follow its rules and keep up with the market before I trust it with real money. This page updates itself every hour.

1

The agent

Three times every weekday it reads the news and the charts, applies a written playbook and places trades through eToro's API. The strategy: about 60% in an MSCI World index fund (about 1,400 companies), held while the market's trend is up and switched to cash when it turns down; the rest in trades on leading stocks and funds from a ranked list of about 340 across the US, Spain, Europe and Asia, each with a stop-loss; idle cash parked in short-term US Treasury bills. Hard limits are enforced in code: no leverage, no short selling, 1.5% of the account at risk per trade (2% maximum), and safety stops at −15% and −25%.

2

The watchdog

Between sessions, a small script with no AI checks prices every 15 minutes and only wakes the AI when something big moves: a position near its stop, or a sharp market move. The AI thinks only when it's worth it, which keeps usage and cost low.

3

The training

Every rule was tested before going live: 60+ years of S&P 500 data, MSCI World since 1979, 500 US stocks and the German DAX, crash tests from 1987 to 2022, and 33 candlestick patterns measured on about 700,000 days of prices. Rules that didn't hold up on new data were dropped. Every week it also does blind chart drills, without knowing the stock or the date, to check it applies its own rules.

How it's judged: a fixed scorecard at 3 and 12 months. Does it follow its rules, keep losses within what the simulations predicted, and keep up with its benchmark, MSCI World (the S&P 500 and Spain's IBEX 35 are shown for reference)? Over weeks, results are mostly luck, so the verdict comes at month 12.
